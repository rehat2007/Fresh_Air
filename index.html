Our Story

Started by two technicians who were tired of bad service calls.

FreshAir Home Services began in 2013 when our founders — both trained HVAC technicians — kept hearing the same complaint from neighbors: no-shows, vague pricing, and repairs that didn't last. So they built the company they wished existed: background-checked technicians, prices confirmed before any work starts, and a workmanship guarantee on every job.

Today our team of 24 technicians serves households and small businesses across Dhaka, backed by proper tools, genuine parts, and a dispatch system that actually keeps appointment windows.
